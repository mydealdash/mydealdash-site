⭐ Easiest: post all 10 top deals as ONE carousel

  1. Press and hold each of the 10 cards below → Save to Photos (scroll sideways to see them all).
  2. Tap Copy caption at the bottom of this box.
  3. Open Instagram, tap + (bottom middle) → Post.
  4. Tap the stacked-squares icon (“Select multiple”) above your photos, then tap the 10 cards in order. Tap the two-arrows icon on the preview so the whole card shows.
  5. Tap Next → Next, press and hold in “Add a caption…” → Paste.
  6. Tap Share. Done!
